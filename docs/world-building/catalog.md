## Colour blocks

Six paintable materials, tinted glass and the glow block. Each is one item; you pick its colour while placing (the colour strip above the hotbar, or , and .).
The palette has 51 colours in 17 families of three shades: white, black, grey, red, orange, yellow, green, mint, blue, cyan, purple, pink, brown, cream, pastel, neon and deep dark. That is 408 different blocks.

## Adding a new decoration

Append one line to the table in src/js/late/99-zzzzzzz-wb2-decor.js: `D(id, name, family, rarity, width, height, placement, interaction, painter, live overlay, light colour, description, extra)`.
- The painter draws the still picture once (it is cached per facing and on / off); the live overlay draws only what moves.
- The interaction is one of the kinds above. A new kind needs a line in SYN.P (its sound), ANIM (how long it plays) and VERB (the prompt).
- Never reorder or remove lines: a decoration’s number is its place in the table and is saved in worlds.
- Then run `node tools/worldbuild-catalog.js` to put it in the store (or give it an EARN goal there to make it a pattern).
