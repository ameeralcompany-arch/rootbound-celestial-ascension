# THE WORLDS WE LEFT BEHIND: story proposal

This is a proposal for you to approve before anything is built. It is built only on what the game already has:
- the Plaza and its eleven townsfolk;
- the first world and the four realms (Inferno, Galaxy, Abyssal Sea, Crystal Realm), each with a world boss and a hidden word;
- the ten dungeons and their bosses;
- Lavi;
- the World Eater.

It invents no menus or systems that would sit unused. Every story beat hangs on something the player already does.

## The main story

Long ago, every world grew from the roots of one great tree. Travelers walked the **Rootways** between them, and each world kept its own songs, festivals and creatures.

Then came **the Great Silence**. Nobody knows why it began. The Rootways went quiet, and the worlds drifted apart. Each world's **name-word**, the word its people sang to call the others, broke into scattered letters. The guardians who kept those words were left alone in the dark, and became dangerous.

You are a traveler who wakes after a hundred years with a strange gift: you can still hear the Rootways. You are not chosen, and not a hero of legend. You simply remember what other people forgot.

The story's thread is the game's own letter hunt. Gathering a world's letters and speaking its word at the Word Gate wakes that world again. Defeating its guardian lets it rest. Clearing its dungeon reconnects its Rootway to the Plaza. Each step visibly brings the world back.

At the very bottom waits **the World Eater**, "Everything That Begins". It is not a god and not evil. It is what the Great Silence became: the universe forgetting itself, swallowing every world nobody remembers. Its existing last line, "Then... let it all begin again", becomes the turn of the ending.

**The message, at the end:** *"Even when worlds drift apart and memories fade, the moments we share still leave something behind. Sometimes, all it takes to bring light back is someone willing to remember."*

## World by world

Each world gets the same structure:

| | |
|---|---|
| Mystery | what is wrong here |
| The guardian | why it became dangerous |
| A local character | |
| An optional story | |
| Restoration | what visibly changes |

**The Plaza, the last crossroads.** The one place where travelers still meet. Its people each remember a fragment of the old Rootways, half as a joke and half as a sorrow. It is the home you return to, and it fills up as worlds wake: lanterns, music, and finally a festival.

**The first world: the meadow, the lake, the cliff.**
- **Mystery:** the flowers here grow in the shapes of letters.
- **Guardian:** Thornmaw the Rootwarden. It wrapped its roots around the world's word to keep thieves away, and you look like a thief. It is a misunderstood protector; after the fight it lets the roots go.
- **Local character:** Hana the gardener, whose seeds won't sprout.
- **Restoration:** colour returns to the grass, fireflies, a warm version of the music, and Hana's garden blooms in the Plaza.

**Inferno.**
- **Mystery:** a forge-world whose fires burn cold.
- **Guardian:** Vulkhar the Pyrelord. It keeps the forges lit alone, out of pride, and refuses help.
- **Local character:** Bram the blacksmith learned his trade here. He is gruff, and secretly sentimental.
- **Restoration:** the forges glow gold, and Bram can hammer one legendary piece's lore into words.

**Galaxy.**
- **Mystery:** stars going out one by one.
- **Guardian:** Nyx the Astral Devourer. It eats dying stars so their light won't be wasted, a grief turned into hunger.
- **Local characters:** Seren the Astral Mage, a scholar of the Silence. Lumi the moth spirit, shy, was born here.
- **Restoration:** new stars, constellations that spell the friends you made, and Lumi's family returns.

**Abyssal Sea.**
- **Mystery:** a drowned city still ringing with old songs.
- **Guardian:** Maelor the Leviathan. It holds the city's last song in its lungs and won't let it go, because it's all that's left.
- **Local character:** Kael the gate guard. His partner was a sailor here and never came back. This is an earned, quiet sadness, not a death: she is found alive, a keeper of the lighthouse.
- **Restoration:** the lighthouse lights, and the music gains a voice.

**Crystal Realm.**
- **Mystery:** perfect, frozen reflections of a festival that stopped mid-dance.
- **Guardian:** Aurelia the Prism Empress, who froze the moment to keep it forever.
- **Local character:** Lulu at the teahouse once danced there.
- **Restoration:** the festival restarts, and its dance comes to the Plaza.

**The ten dungeons, the Rootways' deep roads.** Each dungeon is a buried Rootway, and its boss is what the Silence made of whoever kept it:

| Dungeon | Boss | What the Silence made of them |
|---|---|---|
| Forgotten Ruins | The Ancient Sentinel | still guards a door nobody remembers the reason for ("I am the last door") |
| Crimson Depths | The Infernal Sovereign | a king who burned his kingdom rather than watch it fade |
| Frozen Catacombs | The Frost Empress | she stopped time to keep one hour |
| Dragon's Grave | Varkhazan | the last of a storm-dragon family |
| Eternal Abyss | The World Eater's shadow | the first warning |
| Abyss of the Fallen Star | The Fallen Star | a star that fell trying to reach another world |
| Throne of the World Eater | the World Eater | the end |

## The characters

| Character | Personality | What they want | Their arc |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lavi** (fairy guide) | cheerful, a little bossy, afraid of the dark | to see the whole universe | she can't remember her own world. In the final chapter she learns it was the first one the World Eater swallowed, and chooses to remember it rather than be sad |
| **Mira** (merchant) | practical, funny, haggles with you | a shop on every world | she collects "lost keepsakes" and starts most side stories |
| **Ren** (wandering traveler) | quiet, mysterious, dry humour | to find his sister | he walked the Rootways before the Silence. His sister is found in the Galaxy, working with Seren |
| **Seren** (astral mage) | a scholar, overexplains, kind | to understand the Silence | she gives the lore its words. She admits at the end that understanding mattered less than remembering |
| **Vesper** (fortune teller) | eerie but warm | to see the future again | she hasn't seen it since the Silence (a running joke). Her visions return as worlds wake, and her last one is of a festival |
| **Pip** (village kid) | loud, brave, always wrong | to be an adventurer | the comic heart. He "helps", and in the end really does: he finds the festival's lost bell |
| **Bram** (blacksmith) | gruff, soft inside | to forge one perfect blade | it ties to weapon lore |
| **Hana** (gardener) | gentle, patient | her seeds to grow | her garden is the Plaza's restoration meter |
| **Kael** (gate guard) | dutiful, shy about feelings | his partner home | the Abyssal Sea reunion |
| **Lulu** (teahouse keeper) | a gossip, festival organiser | to dance again | the Crystal festival |
| **Tobi** (baker) | anxious, comic | his bread to be famous | he bakes for every celebration, and burns one every time |
| **Lumi** (moth spirit) | shy, speaks in short lines | to go home | the Galaxy |

Characters remember what you did (bosses beaten, worlds woken, quests finished), so their lines change as you progress. Important lines get voices, made with the free local voice tool already used for Lavi and the bosses. A different voice is picked for each character.

## The emotional journey

| Feeling | Where |
|---|---|
| Happiness | the Plaza's townsfolk, Pip and Tobi's comedy, festivals, the first world's warm music |
| Curiosity | flowers shaped like letters, the drowned songs, Seren's lore, the Rootways |
| Excitement | dungeon entrances and boss films (already in the game) |
| Tension | the bosses' entrances and phases, the world collapse when the last letter is found (already in the game) |
| Sadness, earned, never forced | the frozen festival, Maelor's last song, Lavi's lost world |
| Relief | each restoration: colour, music, people back |
| Pride | bosses, rare rewards, class mastery |
| Belonging | the Plaza filling up, the characters remembering you |
| Hope | the Rootway map lighting up, world by world |

## Building it, in stages (each one tested before the next)

1. **The story layer.**
   - A Journal of short story pages, unlocked by real progress: words spoken, guardians beaten, dungeons cleared.
   - Lavi's story lines at those moments.
   - The religious renames.
2. **The townsfolk.**
   - Progress-aware conversations for the eleven Plaza characters, with voices for the important lines.
3. **World restoration.**
   - Each realm shows its state: Silent, Waking, Restored.
   - Colour, light, music variant and characters change with that state.
   - The Plaza fills up as worlds wake.
4. **Side stories.** Eight short ones on existing systems (worlds, items, characters), for example:
   - Pip's "dragon egg", which is a rock;
   - Kael's letter;
   - Lulu's festival bell;
   - Tobi's recipe from the Inferno;
   - Lumi's lost lantern;
   - Bram's blade;
   - Hana's last seed;
   - Mira's keepsake.
5. **Cinematics.** Using the game's existing film tools:
   - a short restoration scene per world;
   - the final chapter;
   - an ending that lets you walk the restored worlds.
6. **Music.** A restored variant of each world's music, and a festival theme.
