# The thirteen class stories

Every story is an episode in *The Worlds We Left Behind*. Each one happens at the moment the player has earned the evolution. The hero is the player's own character. Each keeper is the one already living at that class's house in the Plaza, and each threat is a guardian or dungeon boss already in the game.

**The shared theme:** a person discovers what their power is actually for, and chooses who to be.

---

## 1. Reaper: *What the Dark Keeps* (built first)

**Premise.** The hero woke a hundred years late, with everyone they loved gone. Every night they carve those names into the standing stones above the Plaza, so someone will remember. Tonight the names begin to fade. The Silence is feeding through the Hollow Grove.

**Characters.** The hero. Lavi, afraid of the dark, who stays anyway. The Hollow, who was here before the Plaza. The Nameless Gardener, who tended the Grove until nobody remembered her.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | To get the names back |
| Emotional weakness | Treats the dark as a thief. Cannot let anything go |
| Relationships | Lavi, the only friend from the day they woke; the Hollow, a teacher who speaks in riddles |
| Defining decision | To stop fighting the dark and walk into it, unarmed |
| The lesson | A reaper does not take. A reaper carries what is falling |

**Scenes.**
1. *The character.* Night on the stones. The hero polishing the scythe, unable to sleep. Lavi being bossy from the safety of the lantern. A name fades on the stone. The Hollow's warning.
2. *The struggle.* The Hollow Grove. The Gardener. A fight the hero loses: every cut makes the dark grow up their arm. A false Gardener bursts into leaves. The Ring of Thorns knocks the scythe away, and the grove's lights go out one at a time.
3. *The breaking point.* Total dark. Lavi, shaking, refuses to leave. A thorn comes for the hero and she flies into it. Her light goes out in the hero's hands.
4. *The awakening.* The hero understands: the dark has been keeping the names all along. They walk in, unarmed. Twelve seconds of nearly nothing. Eyes open. Thousands of lights: every forgotten name. The shadow dresses them, piece by piece.
5. *The reveal.* The evolved form. One cut, the Final Hour, that severs only the roots binding the Gardener. She asks what her name was. The hero, who now knows, whispers it. Lavi's light returns among the others.

**Dialogue.** Hollow: "Do not fear the dark. It is only light, waiting." · Lavi: "It's the middle of the night. Even the crows went to bed." · Hero: "I don't sleep much." · Lavi: "You slept a hundred years. I think you're covered." · Hollow: "A reaper does not take. A reaper only collects what is already falling." · Gardener: "I have no name. Only the grove." · Hero: "Then give back the ones you stole." · Lavi: "It's so dark. I don't... I don't like it." · Hero: "Go. Get out of here." · Lavi: "And let you get lost without me? Please." · Hero: "Lavi. No, no... I still have your name. I still..." · Hero: "A hundred names. Every night I cut at you to get them back. You've been keeping them. Haven't you." · Hero: "I was never taking them. I was carrying them home." · Gardener: "What... was my name?" · Lavi: "Why is it so... oh. It isn't dark at all." · Lavi: "You're still not sleeping, though." · *Final line:* **Nothing that falls is lost.**

**Action.** A running charge through erupting thorns; a root wall cut in two; a parry against converging thorn spears; a spinning sweep that releases Crow Flock; the false Gardener; the lash from behind; a second, angrier charge; the Ring of Thorns.

**Evolution.** Shadow does not smother the hero; it clothes them. Feathers form on the hand, a mantle unfolds, the coat's hem becomes feathered tails, the hood flows forward. The scythe's blade opens to show a night sky inside it.

**Ending.** Dawn on the stones. The names are back, and there is one more than before: the Gardener's. Lavi is asleep on the hero's shoulder.

## 2. Knight: *The One Who Stayed*

**Premise.** When Maelor's grief floods the Abyssal Sea's channel, the lighthouse must burn so the survivors can sail out, and only one person ever knew how to light it.

**Characters.** Kael; his partner at the lighthouse; Maelor the Leviathan.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | Never again be too late |
| Emotional weakness | Believes protecting people means never failing them, so they do everything alone |
| Relationships | Kael, who recognises the mistake because he made it |
| Defining decision | To lower the shield and hold the door open with their own body, trusting others to run |

**Scenes.** 1. Dawn drill at the gate; Kael's letter for the water's edge; a knight who sleeps in their armour. 2. The sea wall. Disciplined sword forms against the tide, until the wall splits and people are left behind. 3. The shield breaks. Kael, holding a rope line, says what he should have said years ago. 4. Silence underwater. The Mythic's feather motif lights one link at a time, each link a promise kept. Armour forms in gold plates that lock with a sound like a bell. 5. The Vanguard stands in the channel. Maelor's song is answered rather than cut. The lighthouse lights at last.

**Dialogue.** Kael: "You've polished that three times. Nobody's coming to inspect it." · Hero: "Then I'll be ready when they don't." · Kael: "I stood at my post the night she sailed. I was very good at it." · Hero: "If I step aside, they die." · Kael: "If you stand alone, they die later." · Hero: "Then run. All of you. I'll be the door." · Maelor: "It is all that is left." · Hero: "I know. Let me hold it with you." · *Final line:* **I stand so that others may rise.**

**Action.** Metal on water: precise, heavy parries that send up curtains of spray; a shield charge along the wall; a single two-handed cut splitting a wave.

**Evolution.** Golden seams spread across cracked armour. Plates lock on in order: greaves, breastplate, pauldrons, helm.

**Ending.** The lighthouse burns, and on the jetty beside it Kael forgets every word he practised, whether this is the night she comes home or one of the evenings after.

## 3. Samurai: *One Look Back*

**Premise.** The Crystal Realm's festival froze mid-dance. Lulu once danced in it. The hero lives by the blade's motto, "Never look back", and has never once visited.

**Characters.** Lulu; Aurelia the Prism Empress; a frozen child mid-laugh.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | To finish things cleanly and move on |
| Emotional weakness | Has never grieved anything |
| Relationships | Lulu, who pours tea and refuses to be left unsaid |
| Defining decision | To look back, sheathe the sword, and join the dance |

**Scenes.** 1. Tea at dusk; a petal falls and is cut before it lands. 2. A silent duel across a hall of frozen reflections, every reflection a version of the hero who stayed. 3. The hero's own reflection will not fight, only asks who they left behind. The blade stops. 4. One look back: spring blows through frozen glass. 5. The Ronin dances through the Empress's guard; one cut, sheathed before the sound arrives. The festival resumes.

**Dialogue.** Lulu: "You never finish your tea." · Hero: "It goes cold." · Lulu: "It goes cold because you leave." · Reflection: "Who did you leave at the bridge?" · Hero: "I don't remember." · Reflection: "Yes, you do." · Aurelia: "I kept it perfect." · Hero: "You kept it from ending. It isn't the same." · *Final line:* **Bloom once. Then remember it.**

**Action.** Stillness then a single blur: contrast of long held stances and cuts lasting two frames.

**Evolution.** Pink light rises through ink-black cloth; a storm of petals becomes a travelling coat; twin crescent crest.

**Ending.** The hero finishes the tea.

## 4. Archer: *Wind Over the Last Seed*

**Premise.** Hana's last seed will not sprout in the Plaza's soil. It needs soil that only exists on a falling island in the Galaxy, and Nyx is eating the stars that keep it aloft.

**Characters.** Hana; Nyx the Astral Devourer.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | To hit what they aim at, from far away |
| Emotional weakness | Keeps a safe distance from everyone, literally |
| Relationships | Hana, patient with things that won't grow |
| Defining decision | To stop calculating, come close, and shoot blind on trust in the wind |

**Scenes.** 1. A perfect shot through a far ring, alone at dawn, with Hana calling them to supper unanswered. 2. Aerial chase between islands; every arrow true and useless against something that swallows light. 3. The wind rips the map away; the seed falls. 4. Eyes closed; wind made visible; hair, grass and stars all lean one way. 5. The Galewarden runs on air. One arrow carries the seed and lands it gently.

**Dialogue.** Hana: "Supper's cold again." · Hero: "I could see it from here." · Hana: "That's what worries me." · Nyx: "They were dying anyway." · Hero: "So is everything. You don't get to eat it early." · Hero: "I can't see it." · Hana (memory): "You don't have to see a seed for it to be there." · *Final line:* **Some things only land if you let them go.**

**Action.** Aerial, wide, with arrows drawing lines across the frame; slow-motion arcs through falling rock.

**Evolution.** Wind wraps as green ribbons; feathers along the bow; a crown of wind-shaped antlers.

**Ending.** A green shoot on a floating island. Supper, eaten at the table.

## 5. Mage: *The Unfinished Proof*

**Premise.** Seren has calculated exactly when the Galaxy's last star dies. The hero is sure there is an answer if they can only finish the equations.

**Characters.** Seren; Nyx.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | To understand everything, so nothing can hurt them |
| Emotional weakness | Treats people as problems to solve |
| Relationships | Seren, who overexplains and is secretly frightened |
| Defining decision | To write a name where the last term should be |

**Scenes.** 1. A floating library of chalk light; two people interrupting each other happily. 2. Spell-circles, beautifully controlled, consumed one by one. 3. Seren's math is correct; there's no way out; she stops talking for the first time. 4. Every symbol stops. The hero rewrites the last line. 5. The Starwright conducts the sky; new stars spell the names of friends.

**Dialogue.** Seren: "Strictly speaking, that's not what 'impossible' means." · Hero: "Then what does it mean?" · Seren: "It means I've run out of paper." · Nyx: "Light is wasted on the living." · Hero: "It doesn't add up." · Seren: "No. It never did. I just liked the company." · *Final line:* **Every word of it was a name.**

**Action.** Controlled: geometry that locks, rotates and fires; no shouting; lines of light racing to their targets.

**Evolution.** Symbols peel from the air and settle as embroidery; a slow ring of squares (never a star-in-circle).

**Ending.** Seren is mid-sentence. The hero is listening.

## 6. Bloodbound: *Better Terms*

**Premise.** Vesper's visions have returned and the first thing she sees is the hero's death in the Crimson Depths.

**Characters.** Vesper; the Infernal Sovereign.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | To pay every cost personally so nobody else has to |
| Emotional weakness | Cannot accept being cared for |
| Relationships | Vesper, eerie but warm |
| Defining decision | To let someone else share the cost |

**Scenes.** 1. Fortune-telling by ember light; a card turns itself. 2. The Sovereign's burning throne hall; each strike costs blood and the hero pays gladly. 3. Nearly empty. Vesper appears where she cannot be and offers her own hand. 4. Red thread runs from hand to hand, then to every person the hero ever saved. 5. The Regent walks through fire unhurt, and offers the Sovereign the same terms.

**Dialogue.** Vesper: "I've seen how this ends. You'll hate it." · Hero: "Will it cost anyone else?" · Vesper: "Only me." · Sovereign: "I burned it so none could take it." · Hero: "How is that going?" · Vesper: "Take it. Don't be noble at me, it's unattractive." · *Final line:* **The price was never meant for one.**

**Action.** Close, brutal, elegant: bloodlight trails, spinning cuts, the wound-glow as a resource we can read.

**Evolution.** Crimson thread stitches a high collar and long gloves; dark leather-like wings of cloth, never feathered.

**Ending.** Vesper reads the card again. It's a festival.

## 7. Elementalist: *Four Old Arguments*

**Premise.** The Frost Empress's stopped hour spreads out of the Frozen Catacombs, and Lumi's own light begins to freeze. She flies into the Catacombs after it.

**Characters.** Lumi, who speaks almost never; the Frost Empress.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | To keep fire and ice apart inside them, in perfect control |
| Emotional weakness | Holds anger and grief in separate rooms |
| Relationships | Lumi, who sits on their shoulder without being asked |
| Defining decision | To stop keeping them apart, and feel both at once |

**Scenes.** 1. Lumi juggles a spark and a snowflake. The hero tells her to be careful. 2. The Catacombs; a precise, alternating duel. 3. The hero's control cracks, both elements collapse, and Lumi freezes mid-flutter. 4. Fire and ice meet in the hero's chest; earth and air answer. 5. The Conduit opens a slow storm of four colours; the frozen hour finally ends.

**Dialogue.** Hero: "Don't mix them." · Lumi: "*chirp*" · Empress: "One hour. It was all I asked." · Hero: "What happened after it?" · Empress: "Nothing. That is why I stayed." · Lumi: "Warm. Cold. Both." · *Final line:* **Fire remembers. Ice forgives.**

**Action.** Colour-coded and readable: orange left, blue right, meeting in the middle at the climax.

**Evolution.** Coat splits into warm and cold halves; four motes orbit.

**Ending.** Lumi, warm again, home on the hero's shoulder.

## 8. Stormcaller: *The Best Crust*

**Premise.** The hero's lightning once burned Tobi's windmill. They've called no storm since. Now Varkhazan, last of a family of storm-dragons, circles the Plaza looking for anyone who speaks its language.

**Characters.** Tobi, anxious and comic; Varkhazan.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | Never to hurt anyone near them again |
| Emotional weakness | Holds back so hard they are useless |
| Relationships | Tobi, who has forgiven it and burns bread every time anyway |
| Defining decision | To stand in the middle of the storm and call it down, trusting themselves |

**Scenes.** 1. Comic bread disaster; a clear sky; a hero who flinches at static. 2. The Dragon's storm; the hero's half-power bolts fizzle. 3. Tobi climbs the ruined windmill to ring it as a lightning rod for the crowd. 4. The hero exhales. Every hair lifts. The storm goes quiet. 5. The Herald walks up a stair of lightning. The two storms greet each other.

**Dialogue.** Tobi: "It's not burned. It's caramelised. Aggressively." · Hero: "I'm sorry about the mill." · Tobi: "Lightning bakes the best crust, I swear." · Varkhazan: "You speak like thunder with its mouth shut." · Tobi: "Do it! I'm terrified! Do it anyway!" · *Final line:* **The sky answers when I call. And I answer when it needs me.**

**Action.** Vertical: bolts as staging lines; hard cuts on thunder.

**Evolution.** Static lifts hair into a crown of spikes; storm-blue coat with a yellow bolt seam.

**Ending.** Burned bread, eaten happily on the rebuilt mill.

## 9. Berserker: *Climate*

**Premise.** In the Inferno, Bram's old forge has gone cold. Vulkhar keeps the last fires lit alone and burns anyone who tries to help.

**Characters.** Bram; Vulkhar the Pyrelord.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | To turn pain into strength |
| Emotional weakness | Pain is the only feeling they trust |
| Relationships | Bram, who says everything with a hammer |
| Defining decision | To stop swinging, put the axe on the anvil, and let it be reforged |

**Scenes.** 1. Bram hands back a broken axe, wordless. 2. Molten duel; the hero grows stronger as they're hurt, and enjoys it. 3. Nothing left to burn; the axe breaks. Vulkhar, equally spent, says he's so tired. 4. Quiet: ash falling like snow. Bram's hammer in the distance, one note at a time. 5. The Warlord doesn't rage. The temperature simply changes. Vulkhar lets someone else hold the bellows.

**Dialogue.** Bram: "Broke it again." · Hero: "It held long enough." · Bram: "That's not the job of a thing." · Vulkhar: "No one else can keep them lit." · Hero: "Have you asked?" · Bram: "Give it here. Sit down. You're bleeding on my floor." · *Final line:* **It stopped being rage. Now it is weather.**

**Action.** Enormous, slow, weighty swings, each landing like a falling building.

**Evolution.** Ash settles as a grey mantle; cracks of ember glow along the skin; a slow-burning crest.

**Ending.** Bram has forged the one perfect blade, and it's a kitchen knife for Tobi. He is proud of it.

## 10. Ninja: *One Footprint*

**Premise.** Ren has heard of one last unmapped road past the Eternal Abyss: the road home he has been looking for. He goes without telling anyone. The hero, who taught themself the same habit, follows.

**Characters.** Ren; the World Eater's shadow.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | To leave no trace, so nothing can be lost |
| Emotional weakness | Leaves first, so they're never left |
| Relationships | Ren, dry, kind, the same disease |
| Defining decision | To stop, be seen, and leave a footprint on purpose |

**Scenes.** 1. Rooftops; a note left, unsigned. 2. A silent infiltration in ink wash; the shadow knows every trick. 3. The shadow erases them from the map; Ren can't remember why he came. 4. The hero dips a hand in ink and presses it to the wall, signing their name. 5. The Phantom is in six places, and every one of them leaves a mark.

**Dialogue.** Ren: "You could have said goodbye." · Hero: "So could you." · Ren: "Fair." · Shadow: "I am only its shadow. Nobody will look for you." · Ren: "Who are you?" · Hero: "The one who came after you." · *Final line:* **There is less of me each time you look. But I left you this.**

**Action.** Ink-wash style: black shapes on paper white, teleports as brush flicks.

**Evolution.** The body thins into brush strokes; a long scarf trails ink.

**Ending.** A real map on the Plaza wall, with a pin in it: *home*.

## 11. Druid: *What Grows Back*

**Premise.** Elder Oak can no longer remember the name of the first world. Thornmaw's roots have closed around the word, and they're also Oak's roots.

**Characters.** Elder Oak; Thornmaw the Rootwarden.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | To protect every living thing, including from change |
| Emotional weakness | Patience as a way of never choosing |
| Relationships | Elder Oak, forgetful and funny and fading |
| Defining decision | To cut the oldest root, with permission |

**Scenes.** 1. Oak asks the hero's name for the third time today. 2. Meadow fight in letter-shaped flowers; the hero only binds, never harms. 3. Thornmaw is strangling the word it swore to keep. Oak asks to be cut free. 4. Spring in a second: every bud on the hill opens at once. 5. Wildheart walks; the forest walks with them. Thornmaw lets the roots go.

**Dialogue.** Oak: "And you are?" · Hero: "Still me, Elder." · Oak: "Good. Stay that way." · Thornmaw: "Thief." · Hero: "I'm not here to take it." · Oak: "A tree that won't lose a branch is already firewood." · *Final line:* **What is cut, regrows. What grows, remembers.**

**Action.** Slow, huge, inevitable; roots as choreography.

**Evolution.** Antlers of wood grow gently; moss cape; flowers open along the seams.

**Ending.** A sapling. Oak asks its name. The hero tells him.

## 12. Gunslinger: *Six Seconds*

**Premise.** The Fallen Star is coming down on a mining town nobody else will defend. Mira has a stall there, and a watch she won't sell.

**Characters.** Mira; the Fallen Star.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | To get in, take the shot, and get out in six seconds |
| Emotional weakness | Everything is a transaction. Never stays |
| Relationships | Mira, who haggles because she's afraid to say anything else |
| Defining decision | To stay past six |

**Scenes.** 1. A haggle over a watch that doesn't work. 2. Six shots, six perfect seconds, and the star keeps falling. 3. The gun clicks empty. Mira opens the watch: it isn't broken, it's stopped at the moment someone didn't come back. 4. The hero sets the watch to zero and stands in the street. 5. The Marshal fires one shot in the light of a falling sun.

**Dialogue.** Mira: "Forty gems." · Hero: "It doesn't tick." · Mira: "Fifty." · Star: "I only wanted to reach them." · Hero: "Six seconds is all I need." · Mira: "And then what?" · Hero: "...I never stayed to find out." · *Final line:* **The law, where there is no one else. For as long as it takes.**

**Action.** Stillness and shots; dust; a long street; the star's reflection in a coin.

**Evolution.** Long coat of gold-trimmed leather; a sheriff-style star (five-pointed, plain) forms from gears.

**Ending.** Mira gives the watch away. It starts ticking.

## 13. Brawler: *Every Blow a Promise*

**Premise.** Pip has followed the hero into the Forgotten Ruins, sure he's helping. The Ancient Sentinel seals the door behind them.

**Characters.** Pip; the Ancient Sentinel.

| | |
|---|---|
| Motivation | Keep moving. Keep hitting. Never be still long enough to feel it |
| Emotional weakness | Afraid of being useless |
| Relationships | Pip, the loud kid who copies everything they do |
| Defining decision | To stop hitting, and hold the door instead |

**Scenes.** 1. Pip's shadow-boxing, always wrong. 2. Thousand-fist fight: fast, funny, then not. 3. The hero's hands break. The ruin is coming down. Pip stands in front of them, tiny, with his fists up. 4. The sun comes up through a crack in the ceiling and lands on two ruined hands. 5. The Champion holds the door open with one arm and throws one punch with the other.

**Dialogue.** Pip: "I'm basically you, but with better hair." · Sentinel: "I am the last door. I will not open." · Hero: "That's okay. I'll hold you up." · Pip: "You always get up. That's why I copy you." · Hero: "Pip. Run." · Pip: "No! That's not the move!" · *Final line:* **Every blow is a promise. This one was yours.**

**Action.** Comic energy turning serious; smear frames and a hundred fists; then one single slow punch.

**Evolution.** Two suns ignite in the fists; red headband; arms wrapped in sunlight.

**Ending.** Pip tells the story wrong, in front of everyone. The hero lets him.
