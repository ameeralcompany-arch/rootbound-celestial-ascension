# Phase 1: audit of the systems this update touches

Checkpoint: git tag `checkpoint-before-ultimate-update`, at 6.13.19 (b29999b). Six read-only audits ran in parallel. Paths are in `src/js/late/` unless stated otherwise.

## Store and weapons
- **What the store sells.** The store (`72-catalog.js` SHOP7, `79-store.js` STORE7, server `server/shop.js`, catalog `server/catalog.json` built by `tools/gen-catalog.py`) has 351 items:

  | Category | Count | What they do |
  |---|---|---|
  | Weapons | 48 | All with combat stats |
  | Gear | 91 | All with stats (`WEAR[id].b` summed by `bonus()`) |
  | Wings | 35 | They change movement: see the cosmetics section |
  | Effects | 35 | Cosmetic |
  | Charms | 15 | Cosmetic |
  | Pets | 16 | Cosmetic |
  | Weather machines | 8 | |
  | Building supplies | 103 | Blocks, seeds and objects |

- **A second weapon store.** The inventory has a local "Buy" (`88-inventory.js:57,76-78`) that spends gems without asking the server.
- **Where weapons come from.**
  - The tables: `WPN` (legacy.html) plus weapon files, the 52 arsenal weapons (`99-zzzzzzzzzz-a0-arsenal.js`, levels 18/26/48/58 against the player level) and 13 Mythics.
  - Each class's list: `CLASS7.C[k].ids`. Class levels: `CLASS7.S.lv`, max 75. Ownership: `own[id]`; the equipped weapon: `wp`.
  - Weapons are also obtained from gun drops (cross-class, 40% from bosses), the arsenal unlocks, the Mythic forge, the dungeon forge, the class starter gift, kill evolutions and trades.
- **What the server knows.**
  - `u.shop.owned` (buys), `u.gear.h` (owned gear reported by the client, rate-limited), `u.enh` (forge levels) and an opaque profile copy.
  - It has no class levels and no weapon table.
- **Rarity risk.** Weapon rarity is read from the catalog (`WPNRAR` in `86-zz-balance.js:16`, `ITEM7.tier`, `a0-arsenal.js:111`). Removing catalog weapons would turn them Common unless their rarity is copied onto the weapons first.

## Cosmetics and the character
- **How the character is drawn.** `AC6.draw` / `drawBody` (`60-char.js:104-213`), about 65 px tall, with fixed layers and the gear parts `GEAR7`.
- **The look.**
  - Stored in `cel.ap`: 10 hairstyles, 4 eye shapes, 6 outfits, 6 accessories and 3 capes, all free and stat-free.
  - Other free visuals: about 84 wings, 6 level auras, 5 store auras, trails, 15 charms, 34 nameplate skins, and 8 emoji emotes with no body animation.
- **Stats.**
  - Every gear piece has stats.
  - Every wing gives a double jump, and flight lift and stamina grow with wing rarity (`85-zz-flight.js:8,15`). That is a movement advantage.
- **What other players see.** Your look reaches them through presence `cx` (at most 600 characters). The client's allow-list `cleanCx` (`20-data.js:179`) seems to drop `ev`, `sk` and `fa`, so remote players may not see your evolution look or skin. This is checked in Phase 2.
- **Collection book.** `BOOK7` (`84-book.js`) exists, but with no preview, no rarity and no equip.

## Breaking and drops
- **The server never sees a hit.** It only receives the final edit that turns a tile into air (`'e'` handler at `server/server.js:234`) and has no terrain, so it can't tell which block was there.
- **All loot is rolled on the player's computer** with `Math.random`. The only random numbers the server makes are dungeon seeds.
- **Eligible breaks.** A trustworthy one is "a natural, never-edited tile became air": `w.edits[k]===undefined` before the edit. Each tile can count once.
- **Fake breaks.** A modified client could fake air edits in open worlds, so the rare roll needs per-account caps per minute and per day, and a distance check.
- **Reward presentation that already exists:**
  - REW8 cards, flashes, reveals and pillars (`92-z-rewards.js`).
  - MOMENT1 (needs a new kind).
  - Server notices `{t:'notice'}`.
  - Soft mining sounds MSFX8 (`86-zz-minesfx.js`).

## The Plaza and the class NPCs
- **How it is built.** The Plaza is generated by code (`76-plaza3.js` PLZ7), 256×128 tiles, with 13 class houses along the street. The server stores no Plaza edits, so new decorations are drawn by code and are identical for everyone.
- **The class masters.** They are in `77-npcs2.js` (NPC2). "Has chosen a class" is `CJOB.cur != null` (`86-z-classjob.js`), and choosing fires the event `rbclass`. The overlay loop at `77-npcs2.js:185-189` is where an arrow belongs.
- **What exists today:** the Sky Run (parkour), 7 Spirit Stones (secrets), one deck chair you can sit on (the sit pose is shown only to yourself), emoji emotes, fishing. There is no fountain any more (`tiles.fill(0)` removed it).
- **Rewards counted only on your computer.** The Sky Run and Spirit Stone rewards add gems locally, even online.

## Dungeons
1. **Stray chests.**
   - CHEST7 (`85-zz-chests.js`) builds its cave-chest list inside `genBase`. A dungeon replaces `gen` with its own map (`p1-dg-world.js:293`), so the previous world's chests survive into the dungeon. They are drawn there (only `cur=='plaza'` is excluded) and can't be opened.
   - The DEPTH8 relic vaults (`86-z-depths.js`) leak the same way.
   - The intended dungeon chests (secret chests and the final chest) are separate and must stay.
2. **Phase entrances in co-op.**
   - The guest is locked outside the boss arena: `startRoom` runs before `pull`, and the boss lock pins the guest where they stood (`r1-dg-coop.js:129`, `p3:20,33`).
   - Dungeon prompts say E, which is now Skill 2, so E doesn't open gates, chests or the exit (F does).
   - A puzzle pad moves only one player.
   - When the guest leaves, the leader stays in duo mode, stuck at gates that need two.
   - Nothing waits for both players before room 1.
   - The partner is pulled by a snap teleport on x only.
   - Reconnect doesn't resync room or boss state.
3. **The dash.** Dash gates, the ice cage and the orb dodge check the old `dash>0`, which nothing sets any more. Since 6.13.18, Shift fires the class dash (STY1), which never sets it. So every gate bounces you back. The dungeon's own dash listener is dead code.

## Sound
- **No crossfades.** Music (MUS7, `87-music.js`) is synthesized live, with no crossfades: fade out, silence, fade in.
- **The class theme sticks**, and any skill flips the music to it.
- **Dungeon music cuts in.** It stops MUS7 without fading it.
- **Uncoordinated music volume.** The owner's entrance plays over the music without ducking it. Five places set the music volume directly.
- **Harsh sounds.** Raw square and saw waves (`snd()`) are used everywhere, including two sounds on every swing.
- **Dull sounds.** Every noise sound passes through a 420 Hz clamp, so they all sound the same.
- **No ambience.** The ambience slider exists, but there is no ambience in normal worlds.
- **Breaking** (MSFX8) is already soft and rate-limited, with no distinct first-hit sound. The pickup is a fixed-pitch blip with no limit.
- **Unused assets.** 1.4 MB of battle voices are unused.
- **Tools.** An offline score engine plus the looping renderer (`tools/movie/login-score.js`) can render new loops.
