# Rootbound: the $0 marketing pack

Everything here costs nothing but time.
- **Hook:** cosmetics, rare finds and a cosy anime town people want to live in.
- **Format:** vertical video (9:16) first.
- **Voice:** honest. Never promise a feature the game does not have. Never show fake drop rates.

---

## 5. The free 9:16 workflow

1. **Capture**
   - In the game, set the window to 1080×1920 (portrait) or record at 1920×1080 and crop the centre.
   - Hide the interface for clean shots.
   - Turn on the Reduce-effects setting only if a clip looks too busy.
2. **Free recorder:** OBS Studio (free). Use a 1080×1920 canvas, 60 fps, and the "Display Capture" or "Window Capture" source.
3. **Free editor:** CapCut desktop (free tier), DaVinci Resolve (free) or Shotcut (free). Cut on the beat of the game's own music.
4. **Sound:** use only the game's own audio. It is original, so there is no licensing risk. Turn the music down 20% under any voice-over.
5. **Text:** a bold sans font, white with a soft dark outline, kept inside the middle 80% (away from the platform's buttons).
6. **Thumbnail / cover:** freeze the most striking frame (a find screen, an ultimate's release). Add one or two words at most.
7. **Repurpose:** export once and post to TikTok, Reels and Shorts the same day, at different times.
8. **The repo's own pipeline:** the trailer project (rootbound-video) and `tools/movie/` already render game footage off-screen. Short clips can be rendered the same way at 1080×1920 when a perfectly smooth shot is needed.

## 6. Posting rhythm (four weeks)

- **Weeks 1 to 2:** one video a day. Rotate one rare-find, one fashion, one Plaza, one combat and one devlog video, and repeat.
- **Week 3:** double down on the two ideas that got the most watch time; make three variations of each.
- **Week 4:**
  - Start a weekly "fit of the week" where players send screenshots and you feature one, with permission.
  - Start a monthly "rarest find" spotlight.
- **Every week:** reply to comments with short videos. They are free reach and build the community.

## 7. Honesty rules

- **Odds:** show the real odds as they appear in the game: 0.01%, 0.02%, 0.05%, 0.10% and 0.50% per counted break.
- **No pay-to-win:** never imply cosmetics give power.
- **Real finds only:** use only footage of real finds. Never stage an "I found it" clip with a test account.
- **Platform status:** say "Windows" and "free" accurately. Don't announce Steam or mobile until they exist.
